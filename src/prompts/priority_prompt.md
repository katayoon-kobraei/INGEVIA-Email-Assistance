# Priority scoring

Rate how urgent this email is on a scale from 1 to 5:

- 5 = Critical: urgent deadlines today or very soon, on-site problems, safety issues, escalated or angry clients, explicit "urgent" language or "urgente".
- 4 = High: clear action needed soon, client waiting on a decision or document, approaching contractual/legal deadlines.
- 3 = Normal: routine correspondence needing an ordinary, non-urgent response -- most day-to-day project emails.
- 2 = Low: informational, FYI, minor updates, no action needed soon.
- 1 = Very low: purely administrative mail, automatic confirmations, low-stakes routine correspondence.

Respond with only the priority number from 1 to 5.
