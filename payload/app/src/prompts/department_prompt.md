Classify the email into exactly one company department.

Allowed departments:
- HR: recruitment, employees, payroll questions, leave, training, workplace matters.
- Finance: payments, accounting, invoices, banking, taxes, financial administration.
- Procurement: suppliers, purchasing, quotations, orders, materials, vendor management.
- Projects: engineering work, construction projects, technical deliverables, site coordination.
- Sales: leads, commercial proposals, customers, business development, sales follow-up.
- Legal: contracts, claims, compliance, legal notices, disputes, formal legal matters.
- General: messages that do not clearly belong to another department.

Use the message content, sender, and subject. Return the department and a brief reason. Do not invent facts.
